\section{Project budget}
The budget for this project is decided by \ac{mdu}. All of the passive electrical components such as capacitors, resistor and pin headers are sponsored by Würth electronics. Components such as \ac{bldc} motors, \ac{mcu}, sensors and additional hardware for the chassi are purchased with our budget. Due to the collaboration with \ac{udea} and their \ac{bom} additional sensors has been included in the budget.

Sponsors for \ac{pcb} manufacturing are still being considered. If no sponsors are found the cost of manufacturing will not be included in the budget due to cheap and reliable manufacturing companies do not exist.

An estimated project budget is shown in Table.\:\ref{tab:estimated_budget}. For a more complete view of the project budget see the preliminary \ac{bom} in Table.\:\ref{tab:bom} available in Appendix\:\ref{appendix:bom}.
