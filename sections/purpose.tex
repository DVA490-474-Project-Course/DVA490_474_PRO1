\section{Purpose}
\label{section:purpose}

The purpose of Multi-robot Soccer - RoboCup is to cultivate international relationships, improve students skills at working in multi culture groups, further the multi-robot system research at \ac{mdu} and contribute to the \ac{ssl}-RoboCup community. Collaboration with people from different cultures is becoming essential in today's increasingly globalized world, as is fostering internationalization. The importance of intelligent systems research can not be underestimated given the clear rise of \ac{ai}, which makes for a perfect opportunity to engage with RoboCup which aims to advance the state of the art for intelligent robots\:\cite{robocup_small_size_league_small_nodate}.
